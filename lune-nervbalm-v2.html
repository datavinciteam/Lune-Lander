Why One Ingredient Is Not Enough for Nerve Pain

Neuropathy is not a single problem. It is three problems happening at once. That is why a cream built around one mechanism,no matter how good that one ingredient is,can only take you so far.

Your nerves rely on blood flow to repair and function properly. When circulation is poor, nerve endings are starved of the oxygen and nutrients they need. Nerve Balm includes capsicum and targeted botanicals that support local blood flow right where you apply it,helping your nerves get what they desperately need to heal.

Inflammation is one of the leading drivers of nerve damage and pain. It is also what makes neuropathy progressive,the longer inflammation goes unchecked, the worse symptoms get. Nerve Balm uses frankincense, arnica, and MSM,ingredients with a long clinical track record for calming inflammatory responses,rather than relying on cheap menthol as the primary active.

When nerves are damaged, they misfire. That is where the burning, tingling, and "pins and needles" come from. Nerve Balm includes CBD/hemp oil, magnesium, and DHA omega fatty acids that work at the nerve receptor level to calm overactive signaling,not just mask it with a cooling sensation that fades in minutes.
